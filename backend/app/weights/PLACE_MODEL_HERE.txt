SonarAI PyTorch Model Weights Directory
======================================

Place your Kaggle-trained PyTorch model checkpoint here:
FileName: best_ssl_unet_accuracy.pth

When best_ssl_unet_accuracy.pth is placed in this folder, the backend's PyTorchInferenceProvider will automatically attempt to load the model checkpoint for real Side-Scan Sonar segmentation inference.

Current Active Provider: MockInferenceProvider (fallback until model checkpoint is provided).
